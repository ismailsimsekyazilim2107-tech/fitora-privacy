Privacy Policy

Home Fitness & Workout

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Your privacy matters. Fitora is designed to provide a personal home workout experience. Workout settings and progress described in this policy are stored locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fitora ("we", "our" or "the App"), published by İsmail Şimşek Yazılım, handles information when you use our home fitness and workout application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Fitora may store:

  • Height and weight entered by the user.
  • Workout history and completed exercise information.
  • Workout duration and estimated calorie information.
  • Fitness goals and selected workout difficulty.
  • Language and application preferences.
  • Sound, voice coach and rest settings.
  • Information about music files selected by the user.

2. Local Data Storage

Fitora uses local device storage to remember application settings, fitness preferences and workout progress. This information is used to provide workout history, progress tracking and personalized application settings.

The app version covered by this policy does not provide a Fitora user account or a Fitora-operated cloud synchronization service.

3. Fitness and Health Information

Users may enter height and weight information. Fitora uses these values for features such as BMI calculations and fitness summaries. Calorie values are estimates and may not reflect actual energy expenditure.

Fitora is a fitness application, not a medical service. Its exercise guidance and calculations are not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent medical conditions.

4. Exercise Videos and YouTube

Fitora may display exercise video guides using YouTube services. When users play these videos, YouTube and Google may collect technical information, IP addresses and video interaction data according to their own privacy policies.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

5. Voice Coach and Text-to-Speech

Fitora can use the text-to-speech functionality available on the device to read exercise instructions and coaching messages aloud. The processing of speech information depends on the device's operating system and selected speech provider.

6. Music Features

Users may select music files stored on their own devices. Fitora uses the selected file location to play the music. The app does not upload selected music files to a Fitora server.

7. Information Sharing

Fitora does not sell locally stored workout history, fitness measurements or application preferences. The app version covered by this policy does not include a Fitora-operated advertising, analytics or payment integration.

Third-party services such as YouTube or the device's speech provider may process information when their features are used. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We rely on the security features provided by the user's device and take reasonable measures when developing the application. Users should keep their devices and operating systems updated.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally remains on the device until it is deleted, application storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backup settings.

Users can manage workout history and application preferences through the available application controls and device settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Fitora is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to create accounts or submit contact details to use its workout features.

11. International Users

Fitora supports multiple languages and may be used internationally. Third-party services may process information in countries where their systems operate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
